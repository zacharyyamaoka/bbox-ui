<Callout title="Status — built, not yet in the registry">
  The inspector exists as two workspace packages and is **live on this site's

  is to copy `packages/panel` (and `packages/inspector` for the tldraw-host
  variant) from the repo.
</Callout>

Two packages share the job:

## `@bbox-ui/panel` — the engine

Host-neutral field resolution: which fields a selection exposes, how multiple
selected instances merge, which tier a field renders in, how presets apply,
and the panel designs that render the result. No Vite, no Next, no Storybook
— it is consumed by the repo's inspector demos and by the
[/create](/create) page, so the two can never drift.

The engine owns the editor kit's defining move: it reads the selection, renders
controls with the [components kit's](/docs/components) vocabulary, and emits
prop mutations — it never reaches into a canvas.

Six panel design variants are built and comparable side by side in the repo's
`demos/inspector`. The `/create` page ships the one that won.

## `@bbox-ui/inspector` — the tldraw-host inspector

The right-hand inspector, main menu and window chrome for a tldraw host,
ported from the tldraw styling lab: style overrides per shape, theme panel,
document name, show-as-stock. It is host-bound by design — the tldraw-side
counterpart of the engine above.

## What "inspector menu" resolves to

The vocabulary list names an *inspector menu*; in the tree that is these two
packages. If you want the pure engine, take `panel`. If you want a working
tldraw inspector, take `inspector` (which brings its own chrome).
